Band bio

Get Sideways is positioned as a party band and 90s/2000s pop-rock tribute/cover act from Birmingham, Alabama. Public booking sources describe the act as built for high-energy, sing-along events with artists/songs ranging from All-American Rejects and Blink-182 to Taylor Swift, Luke Combs, Panic! At The Disco, The Killers, Big & Rich, and more.
